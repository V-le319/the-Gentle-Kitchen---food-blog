---
title: "Spinach Goma-ae"
description: "Blanched spinach tossed in a nutty sesame dressing."
category: SIDE
calories: 90
time: 10
image: "/img/FOOD PIC/images (3).jfif"
ingredients:
  - "1 bunch spinach"
  - "2 tbsp toasted sesame seeds, ground"
  - "1 tbsp soy sauce"
  - "1 tsp sugar"
  - "1/2 tsp sesame oil"
prepare:
  - "Bring a pot of water to boil."
  - "Prepare an ice bath."
steps:
  - "Blanch spinach for 30 seconds, then shock in ice water."
  - "Squeeze out excess water and chop into 2-inch lengths."
  - "Whisk ground sesame, soy sauce, sugar, and sesame oil together."
  - "Toss spinach in the dressing just before serving."
serving: "Serve chilled or at room temperature, in small individual bowls."
---
