---
title: "Cabbage Salad"
description: "Light, crunchy cabbage salad with imitation crab and a citrus-sesame dressing."
category: SIDE
calories: 100
time: 10
image: "/img/FOOD PIC/images (5).jfif"
ingredients:
  - "2 cups napa cabbage, thinly sliced"
  - "4 crab sticks, shredded"
  - "1 tbsp rice vinegar"
  - "1 tsp sesame oil"
  - "1/2 tsp sugar"
  - "Salt, to taste"
prepare:
  - "Slice cabbage thinly and shred crab sticks by hand."
steps:
  - "Whisk rice vinegar, sesame oil, sugar, and salt in a bowl."
  - "Add cabbage and crab sticks, toss well to coat."
  - "Let sit 5 minutes for flavors to meld before serving."
serving: "Serve chilled as a light side or starter."
---
