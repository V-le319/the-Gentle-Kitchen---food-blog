---
title: Asian Meatball
category: MAIN
calories: 800
time: 45
image: /img\FOOD PIC\Korean-Meatballs-13-1024x1536.jpg
description: "Sticky sesame-glazed meatballs over rice, with quick-pickled cucumber."
ingredients:
  - "1 lb ground pork or chicken"
  - "2 cloves garlic, minced"
  - "1 tsp ginger, grated"
  - "2 tbsp soy sauce"
  - "1 tbsp sesame oil"
  - "1/4 cup panko breadcrumbs"
  - "1 egg"
  - "3 tbsp honey"
  - "1 tbsp rice vinegar"
  - "1 tsp sesame seeds"
  - "2 cups cooked rice"
  - "1 cucumber, sliced"
prepare:
  - "Mince garlic and grate ginger."
  - "Mix ground meat with garlic, ginger, soy sauce, panko, and egg."
  - "Roll mixture into small meatballs."
steps:
  - "Bake or pan-fry meatballs until browned and cooked through, about 12 minutes."
  - "Whisk honey, rice vinegar, and remaining soy sauce for the glaze."
  - "Toss cooked meatballs in the glaze until coated."
  - "Sprinkle with sesame seeds."
serving: "Serve over rice with pickled cucumber on the side."
---
