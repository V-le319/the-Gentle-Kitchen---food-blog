---
title: Tonkatsu
category: MAIN
calories: 900
time: 60
image: /img/FOOD PIC/images.jfif
description: "A rustic French vegetable stew, slow-simmered with herbs."
ingredients:
  - "1 eggplant, diced"
  - "2 zucchini, diced"
  - "1 bell pepper, diced"
  - "2 tomatoes, chopped"
  - "1 onion, sliced"
  - "3 garlic cloves, minced"
  - "3 tbsp olive oil"
  - "1 tsp dried thyme"
  - "Salt and pepper, to taste"
prepare:
  - "Dice all vegetables into even, bite-sized pieces."
steps:
  - "Heat olive oil in a pot, sauté onion and garlic until fragrant."
  - "Add eggplant and zucchini, cook 5 minutes."
  - "Stir in bell pepper and tomatoes."
  - "Add thyme, salt, and pepper. Simmer uncovered 25 minutes, stirring occasionally."
serving: "Serve warm as a side, or with crusty bread as a main."
