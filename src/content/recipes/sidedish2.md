---
title: "Miso Green Beans"
description: "Crisp green beans stir-fried with a savory miso-sesame crumble."
category: SIDE
calories: 120
time: 15
image: "/img/greenbean.jpg"
ingredients:
  - "300g green beans, trimmed"
  - "2 tbsp ground sesame seeds"
  - "1 tbsp miso paste"
  - "1 tsp sugar"
  - "1 tbsp vegetable oil"
prepare:
  - "Trim green beans and cut in half if long."
  - "Mix miso, sugar, and a splash of water into a paste."
steps:
  - "Heat oil in a pan and stir-fry green beans until bright green and just tender, about 5 minutes."
  - "Add the miso mixture and toss to coat."
  - "Stir in ground sesame seeds and cook 1 more minute."
serving: "Serve warm as a side to grilled or steamed dishes."
---
