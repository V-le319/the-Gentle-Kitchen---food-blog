---
title: "Honey Roasted Eggplant"
description: "Roasted eggplant halves finished with a honey glaze, sesame, and pomegranate."
category: SIDE
calories: 150
time: 30
image: "/img/FOOD PIC/images (6).jfif"
ingredients:
  - "2 Asian eggplants, halved lengthwise"
  - "2 tbsp olive oil"
  - "2 tbsp honey"
  - "1 tsp black sesame seeds"
  - "2 tbsp pomegranate seeds"
  - "1 scallion, thinly sliced"
prepare:
  - "Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C)."
  - "Score the flesh of each eggplant half in a crosshatch pattern."
steps:
  - "Brush eggplant with olive oil and roast, cut side up, for 20-25 minutes until tender."
  - "Drizzle warm eggplant with honey."
  - "Top with sesame seeds, pomegranate seeds, and scallion."
serving: "Serve warm on a platter as a shareable side."
---
