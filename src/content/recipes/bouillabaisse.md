---
title: "Bouillabaisse"
description: "A rustic French seafood stew, simmered with fennel, tomato, and saffron."
category: "MAIN"
image: "/img/Lobster-Bouillabaisse-WEB.jpg"
calories: 420
time: 45
prepare:
  - "Clean and devein shrimp; scrub and debeard mussels."
  - "Cut fish into large chunks."
  - "Dice onion, slice fennel, mince garlic, chop tomatoes."
  - "Zest the orange."
ingredients:
  - "2 tbsp olive oil"
  - "1 onion, diced"
  - "1 fennel bulb, sliced"
  - "3 garlic cloves, minced"
  - "2 tomatoes, chopped (or 1 cup canned)"
  - "1 tsp saffron threads"
  - "4 cups fish or seafood stock"
  - "1 lb mussels, cleaned"
  - "1 lb white fish fillets, cut into chunks"
  - "1/2 lb shrimp, peeled and deveined"
  - "1 orange, zested"
  - "Salt and pepper, to taste"
  - "Crusty bread, for serving"
steps:
  - "Heat olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Sauté onion and fennel until soft, about 5 minutes."
  - "Add garlic and cook 1 minute until fragrant."
  - "Stir in tomatoes, saffron, and orange zest. Cook 2–3 minutes."
  - "Pour in stock and bring to a simmer. Let simmer uncovered for 15 minutes."
  - "Add fish chunks and mussels. Simmer 5 minutes."
  - "Add shrimp and cook until shrimp are pink and mussels have opened, about 3–4 minutes."
  - "Season with salt and pepper to taste. Discard any unopened mussels."
serving: "Ladle into bowls and serve hot with crusty bread on the side."
