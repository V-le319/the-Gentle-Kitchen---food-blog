---
title: Cream Stew
category: MAIN
calories: 900
time: 45
image: /img/white-stew-close-up.jpg
description: "A gentle Japanese-style white stew with chicken, potato, and carrot."
ingredients:
  - "1 lb chicken thigh, cubed"
  - "2 potatoes, chunked"
  - "2 carrots, chunked"
  - "1 onion, sliced"
  - "2 cups milk"
  - "2 cups chicken stock"
  - "3 tbsp butter"
  - "3 tbsp flour"
  - "Salt and pepper, to taste"
prepare:
  - "Cube chicken and chop vegetables into bite-sized pieces."
steps:
  - "Melt butter in a pot, sauté onion until soft."
  - "Add chicken and cook until lightly browned."
  - "Stir in flour and cook 1 minute."
  - "Add stock, potato, and carrot. Simmer 15 minutes."
  - "Pour in milk, simmer gently until thickened, about 10 minutes."
  - "Season with salt and pepper."
serving: "Ladle into bowls and serve hot with bread or rice."
---
