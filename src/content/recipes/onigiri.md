title: Onigiri 
category: MAIN
calories: 400
time: 30
image: /img/FOOD PIC/images (2).jfif
description: "Hand-pressed rice balls with a savory filling, wrapped in nori."
ingredients:
  - "2 cups cooked short-grain rice"
  - "2 sheets nori, cut into strips"
  - "1/4 cup salted salmon flakes or umeboshi"
  - "1 tsp salt"
prepare:
  - "Cook rice and let cool slightly."
  - "Wet hands with salted water to prevent sticking."
steps:
  - "Place a small mound of rice in your palm, add filling in the center."
  - "Shape into a triangle, pressing gently to seal the filling inside."
  - "Wrap with a strip of nori."
serving: "Serve at room temperature or slightly warm."
