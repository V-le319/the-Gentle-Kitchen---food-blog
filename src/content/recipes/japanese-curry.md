---
title: Japanese Curry
category: MAIN
calories: 900
time: 45
image: /img/FOOD PIC\images (1).jfif
description: "A comforting curry rice with tender beef and root vegetables."
ingredients:
  - "1 lb beef or chicken, cubed"
  - "2 potatoes, chunked"
  - "2 carrots, chunked"
  - "1 onion, sliced"
  - "1 block Japanese curry roux"
  - "4 cups water"
  - "2 tbsp oil"
  - "4 cups cooked rice"
prepare:
  - "Cube meat and chop vegetables."
steps:
  - "Heat oil in a pot, brown the meat."
  - "Add onion, cook until soft."
  - "Add potato, carrot, and water. Simmer 20 minutes."
  - "Remove from heat, dissolve curry roux into the pot."
  - "Simmer 10 more minutes, stirring, until thickened."
serving: "Serve over steamed rice."
---
