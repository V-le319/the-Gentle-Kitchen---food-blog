---
title: Miso Soup
category: SOUP
calories: 110
time: 20
image: /img/FOOD PIC/miso-fish-soup-011.jpg
description: "A light, savory everyday soup with tofu and seaweed."
ingredients:
  - "4 cups dashi stock"
  - "3 tbsp miso paste"
  - "1 block soft tofu, cubed"
  - "2 tbsp dried wakame"
  - "1 green onion, sliced"
prepare:
  - "Cube tofu and rehydrate wakame in water."
steps:
  - "Heat dashi stock until just simmering, do not boil."
  - "Whisk miso paste into a ladle of hot stock, then stir into the pot."
  - "Add tofu and wakame, warm through for 1-2 minutes."
serving: "Ladle into bowls, top with sliced green onion."
---
